Privacy Policy for Mudar App

سياسة الخصوصية لتطبيق مدار

We at Mudar App are fully committed to protecting your personal data privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and process your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Basic Info: Such as email address, name, and phone number when you register or sign in using a Google or Apple account.
  • Location Data: We request access to your geographical location (even in the background) to determine delivery addresses, calculate distances, and track drivers accurately for safety and speed.
  • Camera & Photo Gallery Access: To take or select profile pictures or delivery item pictures for document verification and messaging.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition: To support direct voice streaming and voice commands inside the app.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected data strictly for the following purposes:

  • Operating, maintaining, and improving the transport and delivery services.
  • Connecting drivers/captains to customers precisely and calculating fares.
  • Enhancing the app's performance and ensuring a secure user experience.

3. Account & Data Deletion (App Store Compliance)

In compliance with Apple App Store Guidelines (Guideline 5.1.1), Mudar App allows all users to permanently delete their account and all associated personal data at any time.

Upon submitting an account deletion request, all data associated with you (including name, email, phone number, logs, and any other registered information) will be permanently and irreversibly removed from our servers within a maximum of 7 days.

4. Data Sharing and Protection

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal user data with third parties for commercial or advertising purposes. Your data is protected using high-grade encrypted cloud security standards to prevent unauthorized access.
