Mudar - Account Deletion Request

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data from the Mudar app, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to: omar.muthana.rawi@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject: Account Deletion Request
  3. Include your registered email address in the message body.
What data will be deleted?
  • Your account and all personal data (such as email, favorites, and profile information) will be permanently deleted from our servers.
  • No user data will be retained after deletion.
We will process your request and delete all your data within 7 days of receiving your email.
